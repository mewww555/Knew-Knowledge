Some questions do not stay inside one subject.

A question about a video-game avatar can become a question about identity. A question about a collectible can become a question about sacredness, memory, value, and material culture. A question about psychedelic experience can move between neuroscience, anthropology, philosophy, religion, and the problem of what people mean when they say something felt *real*.

**Knew Knowledge** is a place to follow those questions without forcing them into a single discipline too early.

The site will collect essays, field notes, research ideas, reading trails, and projects about consciousness, culture, virtual worlds, ritual, objects, meaning, and reality.

The point is not to pretend every question has an answer. It is to become better at asking them.

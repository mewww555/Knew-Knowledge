<section class="page-head about-head">
  <p class="eyebrow">About</p>
  <h1>Knew Knowledge</h1>
  <p>Knew Knowledge is an independent interdisciplinary project created by a Social Sciences student interested in consciousness, culture, virtual worlds, anthropology, sociology, psychology, and the ways humans determine what is real and meaningful.</p>
</section>

<section class="prose standalone-prose">
  <p>The project is a place for finished essays and unfinished questions. Some pieces begin with academic research; others begin with a game, an object, a ritual, a strange experience, or a question that refuses to go away.</p>
  <p>The goal is not to flatten different disciplines into one answer. It is to put them into conversation.</p>
</section>
